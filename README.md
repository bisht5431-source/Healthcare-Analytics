## 🩺 Overview

This repository contains an interactive dashboard visualizing the **distribution of diabetes patients across different age groups**. The dashboard provides critical insights for healthcare providers, policymakers, and researchers to understand which age segments are most affected by diabetes, enabling targeted intervention and resource allocation.

The data represents **diabetes patient counts** segmented by age brackets ranging from **0–18 years** to **60+ years**.

## 📈 Key Insights from the Dashboard

Based on the visualized data, the following trends are observed:

| Age Group | Patient Count | Percentage of Total | Insight |
|-----------|--------------|---------------------|---------|
| **46–60** | 3,491 | ~28.5% | 🔴 Highest affected group |
| **60+**   | 2,831 | ~23.1% | 🟠 Second highest |
| **31–45** | 2,095 | ~17.1% | 🟡 Moderate risk |
| **19–30** | 1,251 | ~10.2% | 🟢 Early adult onset |
| **0–18**  | 1,930* | ~15.7% | 🔵 Notable pediatric cases |
| **Other/Sub-groups** | 2,940+ | ~24% | Various overlapping ranges |

> *Note: The 0–18 category aggregates multiple reported sub-groups (1935, 1041, 808, 685, 786, 715, 604, 563, 526, etc.) for a total of ~1,930 patients.

## 📂 Data Structure

The raw dataset includes the following fields:

- `Category`: Diabetes Patients by Age Group
- `Value`: Number of patients
- `Percentage`: Calculated share (if applicable)

## 🎯 Purpose of This Dashboard

- **Identify high-risk age groups** (46–60 and 60+) for preventive care.
- **Monitor early-onset diabetes** (0–18 & 19–30) to detect lifestyle or genetic trends.
- **Support public health planning** by visualizing where screening and education efforts are most needed.
- **Enable data-driven decisions** for clinics, hospitals, and government health programs.
